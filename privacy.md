# Roofing Profit Tool Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 18, 2026**

Roofing Profit Tool is a business planning and calculation tool developed by Ben Sarro.

## Information We Collect

Roofing Profit Tool does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal information or user-entered business information.

Information entered into the app is used to perform calculations and generate results for the user. This information is processed locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

## Accounts and Tracking

Roofing Profit Tool does not require users to create an account or sign in.

The app does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics to track users.

## Third-Party Data Sharing

Roofing Profit Tool does not sell or share user data with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Roofing Profit Tool is a general business utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
